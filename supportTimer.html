Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How do I set my work and break times?

You can adjust the work and break times using the sliders on the settings screen. Once set, these times will be used for your timer sessions.

2. How do I reset the timer?

Press the "Reset" button on the timer screen to reset the timer to the initial settings.

3. Who can I contact for app issues?

If you encounter any issues, please reach out to us via the contact email provided above.
